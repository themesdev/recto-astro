# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First release: the Astro edition of Recto, a blog for Astro and Sanity. The same pages and Studio as the Next.js
edition (1.0.0), so the same dataset works with both, in a design of its own: Geist everywhere (Geist Mono for code),
ink on white with one deep red accent, quiet bands instead of dark ones, 36px controls, type only on the scale with
leading and tracking per size, and a dark mode on the `.dark` class. AI-generated photos for every post, the home page
and the three writers replace the drawings.

Home, essay archive with pagination, posts, about, privacy and thank-you pages; a page builder with nine sections as
native `.astro` components; visual editing in the Presentation tool with `@sanity/astro` and stega, under `/preview/`;
a newsletter sign-up that works without JavaScript; Open Graph images drawn at build time, JSON-LD, sitemap, RSS,
llms.txt and every post as Markdown; static pages on Cloudflare Workers, with the preview and the form in the same
Worker; demo content for an invented journal, Low Season.
